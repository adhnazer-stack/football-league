# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Private football group of friends (10–20 players). Arabic-primary, casual but competitive. They open the app on phones after real-world matches to check standings, submit picks, and argue about who's on top. Context: group chat energy, stadium night, phones in hand.

## Product Purpose

A private-league round management system — track players, record weekly round results, maintain live standings, award achievements, crown an MVP. Success: every user opens it after a round ends and feels the excitement of seeing the updated table.

## Brand Personality

Broadcast-grade. Electric. Earned.
Like being inside a real stadium broadcast — not a startup dashboard, not a fantasy app. Dark, cinematic, OLED-optimized. Neon accents feel like scoreboard LEDs, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- Fantasy sports apps (DraftKings, FPL) — too gamified, too busy
- Corporate SaaS dashboards — wrong register entirely
- Cream/warm neutral landing pages — wrong color strategy
- Generic dark "developer tools" aesthetic — too cold, no sport energy

## Design Principles

1. **Broadcast confidence** — every stat is presented like a scoreboard: bold, unambiguous, large
2. **OLED first** — true blacks, neon-on-black, no gray washes; the screen IS the stadium
3. **Motion is information** — animations communicate state changes (new round, new leader, live status), never decorate
4. **Arabic at home** — layout, type sizing, and spacing treat Arabic as the primary script, English as secondary
5. **Earned glory** — hierarchy is strict: champion/MVP/leader get visual premium treatment the others don't

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. `prefers-reduced-motion` must eliminate all looping animations (pulses, scans, floats) while keeping enter/exit transitions. Arabic RTL layout is the primary layout; English is a secondary locale toggle.
